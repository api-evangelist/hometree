Hometree Group Celebrates Dual National Award Wins for Renewable Heating Excellence Hometree’s mission is to build the UK’s leading residential energy services platform and to close off 2025, two of our installer businesses achieved national recognition for their innovation, engineering excellence, and commitment to sustainable home heating. We’re proud to share two outstanding achievements: IMS Heat Pumps has won Heat Pump Project of the Year at the 2025 H&V Awards. GreenGenUK has secured 3rd place nationally for Renewable Heating Installer of the Year at the 2025 Energy Efficiency Awards.
